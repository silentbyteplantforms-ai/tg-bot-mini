Quick Setup Guide
1
Open Telegram and search for @BotFather. Send /newbot and follow the steps to create your bot. Copy the token it gives you.
2
Search for @userinfobot and send /start. It'll reply with your Chat ID. Copy that number.
3
Paste both above → click Start Bot. Your bot will send you a confirmation message on Telegram instantly.
4
Your bot responds to /start, /help, /id, and /ping out of the box.
